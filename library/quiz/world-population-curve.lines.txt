For all of human history until about 1804, we never reached one billion.
The second billion took a hundred and twenty-three years, until 1927.
Then the billions came faster: three in 1960, four in 1974, and the eighth in 2022,
only eleven years after the seventh.
Not because people had more children, but because far fewer died young.
And the curve is already bending: the United Nations expects a peak of about ten billion in the 2080s.
