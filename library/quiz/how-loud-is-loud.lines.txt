Sound is measured in decibels. A whisper is about thirty, a conversation about sixty.
A lawnmower is around ninety.
A rock concert is about a hundred and ten, and a jet taking off nearby, about a hundred and forty.
But a decibel is not like a centimetre: every ten decibels means ten times the sound energy.
So a conversation carries a thousand times the energy of a whisper.
And a rock concert, fifty decibels louder than a chat, carries a hundred thousand times as much.
Your ears soften the blow: each ten decibels only sounds about twice as loud.
Which is why it is easy to forget that long hours above eighty-five decibels can harm your hearing for good.
Earplugs at the next concert are not a bad idea.
