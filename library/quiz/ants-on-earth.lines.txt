That is twenty thousand trillion, or twenty quadrillion: a two followed by sixteen zeros.
The number comes from a 2022 study that pooled 489 ant counts, from rainforests to deserts.
Shared out, that is about two and a half million ants for every person alive.
Each ant weighs almost nothing, but together their bodies hold about twelve million tonnes of carbon.
That is more than all the wild birds and wild mammals on Earth put together.
And it is a low count: ants that live underground, and whole regions without surveys, were left out.
So next time you see one ant, spare a thought for the other twenty quadrillion.
