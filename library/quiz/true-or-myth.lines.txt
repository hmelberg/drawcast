Five things almost everyone has heard. For each one: true, or myth?
First: you can see the Great Wall of China from space.
[QUIZ] The Great Wall can be seen from space with the naked eye. True or myth?  choices=["True","Myth"]
  [right] Myth. The wall is long but only about as wide as a road, and China's first astronaut, Yang Liwei, could not see it in 2003.
Next, the goldfish, said to forget everything in three seconds.
[QUIZ] A goldfish's memory lasts only about three seconds. True or myth?  choices=["True","Myth"]
  [right] Myth. Goldfish remember for months: they learn when food comes, and which sound means dinner.
Then the old saying that lightning never strikes the same place twice.
[QUIZ] Lightning never strikes the same place twice. True or myth?  choices=["True","Myth"]
  [right] Myth. Tall things get hit again and again: the Empire State Building is struck about twenty-five times a year.
Now the breakfast table: bananas are said to be radioactive.
[QUIZ] Bananas are radioactive. True or myth?  choices=["True","Myth"]
  [right] True. A tiny share of their potassium is radioactive potassium-40, though your body keeps its potassium level steady, so it does no harm.
And last, the octopus, said to have three hearts.
[QUIZ] An octopus has three hearts. True or myth?  choices=["True","Myth"]
  [right] True. Two hearts pump blood through the gills, and a third sends it round the body.
