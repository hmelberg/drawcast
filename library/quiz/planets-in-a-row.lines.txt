About thirty. The Moon is much farther away than most pictures suggest.
But here's the real surprise. Start lining up the other planets: Mercury, Venus, Mars.
Then the giants: Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus and Neptune.
All seven fit in the gap. Just.
It's tight, though. Jupiter and Saturn spin so fast they bulge at the middle, so they have to stand pole to pole, like here.
And when the Moon is at its farthest, they fit whichever way you turn them.
So more than half of that gap is just Jupiter and Saturn.
