Mandarin wins by a mile: nearly a billion people grew up speaking it.
Spanish comes second, thanks to Latin America, with almost half a billion.
Then English and Hindi, close together at around three hundred and fifty million or so each.
French, for all its fame, has only about seventy-five million native speakers.
But count everyone who speaks a language, natively or not, and the order changes.
English jumps to first, with about one and a half billion speakers. Most of them learned it as a second language.
So the world's most spoken language is mostly spoken with an accent.
