[ASK] On Venus, which takes longer: one turn on its axis, or one trip round the Sun?
  [right] The day. Venus takes 243 Earth days to turn once, but only 225 to go round the Sun.

(a) Watch the red marker. Venus goes once round the Sun, and in that time it hasn't quite turned once.

(a) Very. Its ground turns at about six and a half kilometres an hour: walking pace.
(a) And it spins backwards, the opposite way to Earth and almost every other planet.

(a) It does: on Venus the Sun rises in the west and sets in the east.
