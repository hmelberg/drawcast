By 2021 it was about seventeen thousand tonnes: a fall of ninety-nine percent.
Why 1989? That year the Montreal Protocol came into force, to phase out CFCs from fridges and spray cans.
In time every country in the United Nations signed it, the first treaty ever to manage that.
They moved fast because, in 1985, scientists had found a hole in the ozone over Antarctica, letting through the ultraviolet light that causes skin cancer.
And it worked: around 2000, the hole stopped growing.
But the old gases linger in the sky for decades. The hole over Antarctica should heal only around 2066.
So a few years of action stopped the damage, but the sky needs another forty years to heal.
