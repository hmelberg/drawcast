# The 50 topics (numbers are from memory — VERIFY on OWID; OWID wins)

Form/shape are suggestions. D = DIALOGUE (two voices).

## Group A
1. `children-in-1800` — In 1800, how many children died before their 5th birthday? (~43 % worldwide; today ~4 %). Scale guess. Twist: in 1800 even the richest country was no better than the worst-off country today.
2. `poverty-headline` D — How many people left extreme poverty EVERY DAY, 1990–2015? (~130,000/day — Max Roser's "headline newspapers could have run every day for 25 years"). Typed number. Shape 9.
3. `deaths-per-energy` — Rank coal, oil, gas, nuclear, solar by deaths per TWh of electricity (accidents + air pollution; coal ~25, oil ~18, gas ~3, nuclear ~0.03, solar ~0.02). Rank cards. Twist: nuclear ≈ solar; Chernobyl and Fukushima included.
4. `wild-mammals-share` — Of all mammals on Earth by weight, what share is wild? (~4 %; humans ~34 %, livestock ~62 %). Pie slice. Twist: humans alone outweigh wild mammals ~10×.
5. `guinea-worm` — Guinea worm: 3.5 million cases in 1986. How many in 2023? (14 human cases). Typed number or scale. Twist: no vaccine, no cure — water filters and behaviour; Carter Center.
6. `rwanda-parliament` D — Which country has the highest share of women in parliament? (Rwanda ~61–64 %). Choose among cards/countries. Shape 4.
7. `bee-colonies` — Myth or true chain: "Honeybee hives worldwide have declined since 1961" (MYTH — managed hives up strongly), plus 2 other bee/insect claims you can verify on OWID. Shape 3.
8. `space-launches` — Objects launched into space in 2023 vs ALL years 1957–2000 combined? Compare higher-or-lower. Twist: Starlink.
9. `price-of-light` — How many hours of work bought an evening's light in 1800 vs today (Nordhaus via OWID). Shape 9 or 2.
10. `literacy-1820` — Share of the world able to read in 1820 (~12 %) vs today (~87 %). Scale guess; twist: absolute number of illiterate people has fallen too (verify).

## Group B
11. `livestock-land` — Livestock uses ~77 % of farmland. What share of the world's calories does it supply? (~18 %). Scale or pie. Shape 7.
12. `local-food-myth` D — Share of a beef burger's emissions from transport? (<1 %; for food overall ~6 %). Bar ask. Twist: what you eat beats where it's from.
13. `ocean-plastic-country` — Which country puts the most plastic into the ocean? (Philippines ~1/3, per Meijer et al. 2021). Choose. Twist: small rivers, not big ones; mismanaged waste.
14. `plastic-recycled` — Share of all plastic ever made that has been recycled (~9 %). Scale. Shape 1.
15. `smallpox-toll` — Smallpox deaths in the 20th century vs deaths in both world wars (~300 million vs ~ 80–100 million). Compare. Twist: eradicated 1980, the first.
16. `measles-deaths` — Draw the rest of the line: measles deaths 1980 (~2.6 M?) → recent (~100 k). Shape 2.
17. `tb-still-top` D — Which infectious disease kills the most people today? (tuberculosis ~1.25 M, back on top after COVID). Choose.
18. `women-outlive-men` — In how many countries do women live longer than men? (all). Typed/choose. Twist: why (biology + behaviour), the gap's size.
19. `dutch-height` — The Dutch are the world's tallest men. Were they 150 years ago? (no — they grew ~15–20 cm). Shape 2 or 7.
20. `fertility-halved` — Average births per woman worldwide 1960 vs today (~5 → ~2.3). Scale. Twist: South Korea ~0.72.
