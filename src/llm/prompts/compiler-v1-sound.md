- **play** sounds synthesized notes — ONLY when the figure is genuinely about sound or music (a scale on a staff, a chord on a keyboard, an interval ratio): `{"play": "C4:q E4:q G4:h", "tempo": 100, "instrument": "piano", "speak": "…"}`. Notation: space-separated notes, pitch letter + optional #/b + octave (C4, F#3, Bb4), duration suffix `:w/:h/:q/:e/:s` = 4/2/1/½/¼ beats (default q), chords joined with `+` (`C4+E4+G4:h`), `R` for a rest. Instruments: tone (default), piano, organ, pluck, bell, strings, flute, marimba. Up to four parallel voices as `"play": [{"notes": "…", "instrument": "piano"}, {"notes": "…", "instrument": "pluck"}]` start together (melody over bass). A whole KNOWN TUNE is best written as ABC: `"play": {"abc": "M:4/4\nL:1/4\nQ:1/4=120\nK:C\nC C G G | A A G2 |"}` — tempo comes from Q:, and note_sheet takes the same tune via its `abc` param, so figure and sound share one source. Two sync lists tie ink to sound, both mapping id k to the k-th sounding note of the first voice: `reveal` ids appear at their note's start and STAY (`"reveal": ["note_0", "note_1"]` — staff notes accumulating), `press` ids go down at the start and BACK UP at the note's end (`"press": ["key_0", "key_1"]` — piano keys pressed and released). Combine them: reveal the staff notes while pressing the keys. Pair play with the matching visual (note_sheet, piano_keys) so viewers SEE what they hear; never decorate an unrelated figure with sound.
- **music** draws a standard music symbol from a real music font — `{"id": "q", "type": "music", "symbol": "eighth_note", "x": 300, "y": 400}` — notes (whole/half/quarter/eighth/sixteenth_note, `stem`, `dots`), rests, treble/bass/alto_clef, sharp/flat/natural, p…ff, fermata, `time` with `"time": "3/4"`; `size` is one staff space (default 26). Use it for symbols on their own (a gallery of note values, a clef explained); for notes on a staff use note_sheet, whose `clef` is treble, bass, or grand (two staves joined by a brace: `notes` on top, `bass_notes` below, lined up by beat — piano music).
